# Methods paragraph (research prototype)
A reproducible prototype pipeline was evaluated using the CC BY4.0 multi-channel surface-EMG dataset of Ebied et al. (2020; DOI10.1109/NILES50944.2020.9257909; dataset DOI10.5281/zenodo.5189275). Eight-channel EMG was converted to a causal RMS effort envelope, resampled to10Hz, normalized to an early-task reference, and segmented into5-second windows at1-second stride. Early/late task intervals supplied explicitly weak temporal targets, not biological fatigue ground truth. Participants were partitioned into9 training,3 validation and3 untouched test subjects before fitting normalization. Logistic regression, gradient boosting and small GRU/LSTM/CNN models were compared; neural training used five fixed seeds, train-only augmentation and validation-based early stopping/calibration. ONNX exports were checked against PyTorch and the supported CNN was executed in Unity2022.3.20f1 using Sentis2.0.0. These experiments validate engineering paths only; no load-cell participant data or clinical fatigue accuracy was established.
